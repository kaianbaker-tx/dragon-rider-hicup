# Dragon Rider Hicup

**Who you are:** a dragon rider named Hicup
**What you do:** fly, and your dragon shoots fire
**How you win:** beat the alpha dragon at the end
**How you lose:** get hit 3 times and you fall off the dragon
                  (a 1up and the dragon catches you)
**Gets harder:** each level has more dragons and they get faster
**First thing you see:** the button that says PLAY
**Feels like:** epic
